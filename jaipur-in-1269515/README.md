Dataset: Air Quality Dataset for Jaipur

About Dataset

This dataset contains historical air quality time series for Jaipur covering 2022-08-01 to 2026-02-18. It includes key air pollution indicators such as AQI indices and major pollutants, plus city metadata including coordinates, administrative areas, and population. Use this dataset to analyze long-run air quality trends in Jaipur, compare Jaipur with other cities, and build models that link air quality to urban characteristics. Air quality values are retrieved from the Open-Meteo Air Quality service using the cams_global domain, and city metadata is derived from the GeoNames city list. Nitiraj Kulkarni

Files

city_info.csv contains city identifiers, coordinates, admin areas, and population.

air_quality_historical.csv contains the historical time series.

data_dictionary.csv describes each column, units, and meaning.
